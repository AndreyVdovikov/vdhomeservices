How to Find a Good Handyman in Wesley Chapel, FL

Where to look, what to check before you hire and how to avoid the usual problems.

By Andrew · Updated November 14, 2026

Finding a handyman in Wesley Chapel is easy. Finding a good one, who shows up when he says, does the job he quoted and cleans up after, is harder. I'm Andrew, I'm a handyman here, and this is the checklist I would use if I were hiring someone for my own house.

Where to look

Start with Google Maps. Search "handyman near me", open a few profiles and look at how many reviews each one has and how recent they are. A 5.0 with six reviews tells you less than a 4.7 with twenty-six. Your neighbors are the second best source, and the fastest way to get a straight answer. Neighborhood Facebook groups and Nextdoor are full of people asking who fixed their fence or hung their TV, and what it cost, and in Epperson, Wiregrass Ranch and Meadow Pointe that conversation never stops. Marketplaces like Thumbtack and Angi are useful for comparing quotes, as long as the reviews belong to the person who will actually come to your house.

What to check before you hire

Look for a real name and a phone number that someone answers. If you cannot reach a person before the job, you will not reach them after it. Reviews with details are worth more than praise: "great job" is fine, but "hung six pictures and a heavy mirror, everything was level" tells you something. Look at the photos too, and make sure they show real jobs in real houses and not stock pictures. Then ask for a clear estimate before the work starts, in writing or in a text, so you both know what is included.

The last thing is honesty about what the handyman does. A good one tells you when a job is not for him. In Florida, electrical, plumbing and A/C work must go to a licensed contractor, and you can check any license on the state's DBPR website. I don't do those jobs, and I will say so.

Red flags

Be careful with anyone who asks for a large payment up front for a small job, who will not give you a price, even a range, and pushes you to decide right now, or who has no reviews, no photos and no address anywhere. A quote far below everyone else's, with "extras" showing up later, is another warning sign. And someone who says yes to every job, including the ones that need a licensed pro, is telling you more about himself than about the work.

How pricing works

Handymen charge either by the job or by the hour. I give a free estimate for the job, so you know the number before I start. What changes the price is how many items there are, how big and heavy they are, what the wall or surface is made of (drywall, brick, tile, stucco), and how hard the spot is to reach. The fastest way to get an estimate is to text me a photo of the job. It takes you a minute and tells me most of what I need.

Get more from one visit

Most homeowners have a short list of small things that never get done, so write it down before the visit. I can often do several in one trip, such as hanging pictures and mirrors, mounting a TV, shelves and curtain rods, assembling furniture, patching drywall, repairing a deck or a fence, or painting a room. Moving into a new house is the best time for this, with boxes, empty walls and a long list, and I can do the whole thing in one visit.

FAQ

How do I find a good handyman near me?

Start with Google Maps and your neighborhood groups. Look for a real name and phone number, a good number of recent reviews, photos of real work and a clear estimate before anything starts.

How much does a handyman cost in Wesley Chapel?

It depends on the job: what it is, how big it is, the wall or surface, and how hard it is to reach. I give free estimates, and the easiest way is to text me a photo of the job at (656) 231-1836.

What jobs can a handyman do?

Hanging pictures and mirrors, mounting TVs and shelves, assembling furniture, patching drywall, painting, fixing doors, fences and decks, and similar small and medium repairs around the house.

Can a handyman do electrical or plumbing work?

Electrical, plumbing and A/C work needs a licensed contractor in Florida. I don't do those jobs. You can check any contractor's license on the Florida DBPR license search.

Do I need to be home during the job?

For most jobs it helps if you are home at the start, so we agree exactly what to do and where. After that, many small jobs go fine without you standing next to me.
