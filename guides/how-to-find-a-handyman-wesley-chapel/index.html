How to Find a Good Handyman in Wesley Chapel, FL

Where to look, what to check before you hire and how to avoid the usual problems.

By Andrew · Updated November 14, 2026

Finding a handyman in Wesley Chapel is easy. Finding a good one, who shows up when he says, does the job he quoted and cleans up after, is harder. I'm Andrew, I'm a handyman here, and this is the checklist I would use if I were hiring someone for my own house.

Where to look

Google Maps

Search "handyman near me" in Google Maps and open a few profiles. Look at the number of reviews and how recent they are. A 5.0 with six reviews tells you less than a 4.7 with twenty-six.

Your neighbors

Neighborhood Facebook groups and Nextdoor are the fastest way to get a straight answer. Ask who fixed their fence or hung their TV, and what it cost. People in Epperson, Wiregrass Ranch and Meadow Pointe talk about this all the time.

Online marketplaces

Thumbtack, Angi and similar sites are useful for comparing quotes. Just check that the reviews belong to the person who will actually come to your house.

What to check before you hire

  • A real name and a phone number that someone answers. If you cannot reach them before the job, you will not reach them after it.
  • Reviews with details. "Great job" is fine. "Hung six pictures and a heavy mirror, everything was level" is better.
  • Photos of real work. Not stock photos, but jobs in actual houses.
  • A clear estimate before the work starts. In writing or in a text, so you both know what is included.
  • Honesty about what they do. A good handyman tells you when a job is not for him. In Florida, electrical, plumbing and A/C work must go to a licensed contractor, and you can check a license on the state's DBPR website. I don't do those jobs, and I will say so.

Red flags

  • A large payment up front for a small job.
  • No price, not even a range, and pressure to decide right now.
  • No reviews, no photos and no address anywhere.
  • A quote far below everyone else's, with "extras" showing up later.
  • Someone who says yes to every job, including ones that need a licensed pro.

How pricing works

Handymen charge either by the job or by the hour. I give a free estimate for the job, so you know the number before I start. What changes the price: how many items there are, how big and heavy they are, what the wall or surface is made of (drywall, brick, tile, stucco) and how hard the spot is to reach.

The fastest way to get an estimate is to text me a photo of the job. It takes you a minute and tells me most of what I need.

Get more from one visit

Most homeowners have a short list of small things that never get done. Write it down before the visit. I can often do several things in one trip, such as:

  • Hanging pictures and mirrors
  • Mounting a TV, shelves and curtain rods
  • Assembling furniture
  • Patching drywall
  • Repairing a deck or a fence
  • Painting a room

Moving into a new house is the best time for this. Boxes, empty walls and a long list: I can do the whole thing in one visit.

FAQ

How do I find a good handyman near me?

Start with Google Maps and your neighborhood groups. Look for a real name and phone number, a good number of recent reviews, photos of real work and a clear estimate before anything starts.

How much does a handyman cost in Wesley Chapel?

It depends on the job: what it is, how big it is, the wall or surface, and how hard it is to reach. I give free estimates, and the easiest way is to text me a photo of the job at (656) 231-1836.

What jobs can a handyman do?

Hanging pictures and mirrors, mounting TVs and shelves, assembling furniture, patching drywall, painting, fixing doors, fences and decks, and similar small and medium repairs around the house.

Can a handyman do electrical or plumbing work?

Electrical, plumbing and A/C work needs a licensed contractor in Florida. I don't do those jobs. You can check any contractor's license on the Florida DBPR license search.

Do I need to be home during the job?

For most jobs it helps if you are home at the start, so we agree exactly what to do and where. After that, many small jobs go fine without you standing next to me.
