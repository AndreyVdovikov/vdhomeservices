Handyman subscription

One handyman you know, who keeps the small stuff from piling up. Three monthly plans for ongoing repairs and home upkeep in Wesley Chapel and nearby, starting at $99 a month. Opening soon: join the waitlist.

Why a subscription

Most homes do not need one big repair. They need a steady trickle of small ones: a loose door, a TV to mount, a shelf, a patch of paint. People put these off because finding someone each time is a hassle. A subscription is meant to make it simple: one number to text, one person who already knows your home.

Who it is for

  • Busy homeowners who would rather text a photo than hunt for a handyman every time.
  • Families helping relatives. Arrange regular help for a parent or family member nearby or far away, so small repairs do not pile up.

What it would cover

Questions

Is the handyman subscription available now?

It is opening soon. Join the waitlist: call or text (656) 231-1836 and tell me what you would want covered, and you will hear first when it starts.

What counts as a visit?

A visit is one trip to your home with the hours of work included in your plan. The hours are what matter. On the larger plans you can split them into several shorter visits, or use them for one bigger job.

What jobs are covered?

Everyday small repairs and installs, the same work I do now: TV mounting, picture hanging, furniture assembly, door and drywall fixes, painting touch-ups and similar jobs. I also take a look at problems and tell you what needs doing. Work that legally needs a licensed electrician or plumber is not part of it.

What if a job is bigger than my plan?

Tell me about it first. I will say how many hours it needs and whether it fits your plan, or give you a free estimate for the extra time. No surprises.

Can I set it up for a parent or relative?

Yes, that is one of the main reasons for the plan. You can arrange regular help for someone who lives nearby or far away, so small repairs do not pile up.

Can I still book a single job?

Of course. One-time jobs work exactly as before: send a request or text a photo and you get a free estimate.
