FAQ

Questions about gifts

How does a gift certificate work?

You buy a certificate for $145, $285 or $478. Right after payment you fill in a short form with the address where the work will be done and what help is needed, and I contact the person to schedule a time. It works like a regular request, except the work is already paid for.

Who fills in the form, me or the person receiving the gift?

Either. You can fill it in right after you pay, or pass the link to the person you are gifting and they fill in their own address and details.

What can the certificate be used for?

Everyday handyman work: TV and shelf mounting, picture hanging, furniture assembly, door and drywall repairs, painting touch-ups and similar jobs. Work that legally needs a licensed electrician or plumber is not included.

What if the job costs more than the certificate?

I tell you what the job needs before I start. If it costs more, the difference is agreed in advance. No surprises.

Is this a good gift for a parent or an older relative?

Yes, it is one of the main reasons for it. It helps someone who needs a hand around the house without them having to look for a handyman or ask for favors.
