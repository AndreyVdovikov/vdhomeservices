Black Slat Accent Panels on a Kitchen Island in Wesley Chapel

By Andrew · 2026-11-07

A kitchen island is the first thing people see when they walk in, and a plain white side can feel flat. On this project I covered it with black slat accent panels.

The dark slats make a clean, modern contrast against the white countertop. The panels are easy to customize, so they can be cut to fit the size and shape of your island, a peninsula or any other plain wall.

It is a simple upgrade, and it changes how the whole kitchen looks without a full remodel.

Quick question

Can slat panels go on a kitchen island?

Yes. They can be cut to the height and length of the island and finished to match your cabinets and countertop. Send me a photo and the measurements for a free estimate.
