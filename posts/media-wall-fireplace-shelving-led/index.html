Media Wall with Custom Shelving, LED Accents and a Fireplace Feature

By Andrew · 2026-05-28

Another media wall finished in the Tampa Bay area. This one included custom shelving, LED accent lighting, a custom mantel and a full fireplace feature wall.

A lot of the work in a project like this is in the details. The lines, the spacing and the lighting all have to be right for the wall to look clean and modern, so I take my time on layout before anything is fastened.

Thank you to the homeowners for trusting me with it.
