Welcome

Hi, I'm Andrew, your local handyman in Wesley Chapel, FL

Thanks for stopping by! I'm Andrew, the owner of Andrew Handyman Services. I help homeowners in Wesley Chapel and nearby communities, including Land O' Lakes, Zephyrhills, Dade City, New Tampa, Lutz and Odessa, keep their homes looking great and working the way they should.

Whether it's mounting a TV, patching drywall, repairing rotted wood, painting a room, refreshing your kitchen cabinets or fixing a fence or deck, I treat every job like it's my own home. You get a clear estimate before I start, honest advice (including when something isn't worth doing) and a clean finish when I'm done.

Don't just take my word for it: read what customers say on Google, Facebook, Yelp and Thumbtack below. Send a quick request with the form at the top of the page, or call or text (656) 231-1836, and I'll get back to you within a few hours. I'm happy to help!
