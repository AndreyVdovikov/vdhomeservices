Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost to hang pictures?

Estimates are free. The price depends on how many pieces there are, how big and heavy they are and what the wall is made of. Text me a photo at (656) 231-1836 and I will give you a clear number.

Can you hang a heavy mirror?

Yes. I check the wall, pick anchors rated for the weight and hang it level. If a wall is not a good place for something that heavy, I tell you before I drill.

Can you plan a gallery wall for me?

Yes. We choose the layout together, I mark it out and then hang everything level with even spacing. If you already have a picture in mind, I can follow it.

Will you fill the old nail holes?

Small nail holes can be patched when I remove old hangers. If you want paint matched, I can do that too through my drywall and painting services.

Can you hang pictures on brick, block or tile walls?

Yes. These need different bits and anchors than drywall, and I use the right ones so the wall is not damaged.
