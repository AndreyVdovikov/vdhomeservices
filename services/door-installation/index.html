Questions

Good to know

How much does door installation cost?

Estimates are free. The price depends on the type of door (French, patio, entry or interior), the size of the opening and what trim or repair is needed around it. Call or text (656) 231-1836 and I will give you a clear number.

Do I buy the door myself?

You can bring your own door or I can help you choose one that fits the opening. I confirm the size before ordering, so there are no surprises.

Can you install a French door where there is only a window or wall?

Cutting a new opening in a wall is structural work. I tell you honestly what I can do and when you need a contractor for that part.

Do I need a permit to replace a door?

Replacing a door in the same opening usually does not, but rules depend on your city or county and the type of door. I tell you upfront what applies before we start.

Can you fix a door that sticks or will not close?

Yes. Most sticking doors come down to hinges, the strike plate or swollen wood, and are a quick fix. I look at it and tell you what it needs.
