Questions

Good to know

Do I need to buy the mount myself?

You can supply your own bracket, or I can point you to a mount that fits your TV and wall. I confirm everything before I start so there are no surprises.

Can you mount a TV on any wall?

Most drywall, brick and stucco walls work well. I check what is behind the wall first and tell you upfront if a wall is not a good fit.

How long does it take?

A standard TV mount is usually done within an hour or two. Shelves and curtain rods are quicker, and larger jobs are quoted ahead of time.
