Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost to mount a 65 inch TV?

Estimates are free. The price depends on the size of the TV, the wall (drywall, brick or stone) and whether you want the wires hidden. Call or text (656) 231-1836 and I will give you a clear number.

Do I need to buy the mount myself?

You can supply your own bracket, or I can point you to a mount that fits your TV and wall. I confirm everything before I start so there are no surprises.

Can you mount a TV above a brick or stone fireplace?

Yes. I check the surface and use the right anchors. If a fireplace is too hot or the wall is not a good fit, I tell you before I start.

Can you mount a TV outside on a lanai?

Yes, on a suitable wall with a bracket and TV that are made for outdoor use. Call me with the details of your lanai.

Can you hide the wires?

I hide the cords in a cord cover or route them behind the wall with a cable pass-through kit. I do not install new outlets.

How long does it take?

A standard TV mount is usually done within an hour or two. Larger screens, fireplaces and wire concealment take longer, and I tell you the time ahead.
