What I do

Curtain, Blind & Shade Installation done right

  • Curtain and drapery rods mounted into studs or with the right anchors, level and at the right height
  • Extra-wide and heavy drapery rods with center supports so they do not sag
  • Mini blinds, faux wood blinds and cellular shades mounted inside or outside the window frame
  • Roller shades, Roman shades and cordless blinds installed and adjusted

  • Old brackets, rods and blinds removed and the holes patched
  • Blinds shortened or adjusted to fit, where the product allows it
  • Measuring help before you order, so the blinds and rods you buy actually fit

Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost to install blinds or curtain rods?

Estimates are free. The price depends on how many windows there are, how wide they are and what the walls are made of. Text a photo of the window to (656) 231-1836 and I will tell you what to expect.

Can you install blinds I bought online or at a big-box store?

Yes. Most people do. Bring the blinds as they are, and I install them and adjust the fit. If something arrives the wrong size, I tell you before I start so you can exchange it.

Can you hang heavy curtains on a long rod?

Yes. Long and heavy rods need a center support and strong mounting points, and I plan the brackets around the studs so the rod stays straight under the weight.

Do you install motorized shades?

I mount the shade itself and the brackets. I do not do electrical work, so if a motorized shade needs power that is not already there, a licensed electrician adds that part.

Can you cut or shorten blinds?

Some blinds can be adjusted or shortened and some cannot. I check the product first and tell you what is possible before I touch it.

Do you hang curtains or only the rod?

Both. I mount the hardware, hang the panels, and check the length so they clear the floor the way you want.
