Questions

Good to know

Who owns the lights?

You do. Unlike companies that rent lights and take them back, the lights we install are yours, and we store them for you after take-down.

When do you take the lights down and where do they go?

We take them down in January and store them until the next holiday season, ready to be installed again.

Can I choose the colors?

Yes. Warm white, cool white, multicolor or any color mix that you like. Tell me what you have in mind and we will plan it together.

How do I get an estimate for holiday lighting?

Estimates are free. The price depends on the size of your home and the areas you want lit, so call or text (656) 231-1836 and I will take care of it.

Do you also serve Land O' Lakes?

Yes. See my Christmas light installation page for Land O' Lakes, or call to check your address.
