Questions

Good to know

Will the patch be visible afterwards?

I match the surrounding texture and prime the repair so it blends in. For a flawless look I can also paint the wall, so the repair disappears completely.

Can you fix water-damaged drywall?

Yes, once the source of the leak is repaired. I cut out the damaged section, patch and finish it. If I see an active leak or mold I will tell you before starting.

Do you paint after the repair?

Yes. Painting is available as part of the same visit, which saves you a second trip and a second appointment.
