Questions

Good to know

How much does furniture assembly cost?

Estimates are free. The price depends on what you bought, how many pieces there are and how complicated they are. Text me the list or a photo of the boxes at (656) 231-1836.

Can you assemble furniture I bought online?

Yes. IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon, Costco and other flat-pack brands are all fine. I just need the boxes delivered to the house.

What if parts are missing?

I check everything against the instructions before I start. If something is missing, I tell you right away so you can ask the manufacturer for the part, and I build the rest.

Can you anchor a dresser or bookcase to the wall?

Yes. Anchoring tall furniture is a good idea, especially with kids or pets in the house. I use the right anchors for the wall.

Do you assemble outdoor furniture?

Yes. Patio sets, benches and outdoor storage are fine.
