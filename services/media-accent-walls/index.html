Freshen up your living room with a custom wall

A living room can feel tired even when nothing is broken. The fastest way to change the whole mood of a room is a single feature wall. Wall panels do that better than paint alone: they add texture, depth and a clean, finished look that you notice the moment you walk in.

I design and install custom accent walls with wood slats, paneling and shiplap, and I finish them in the color that suits your room. White, black, warm wood, dark stain or something in between: we pick it together, and I can match what you already have. If you are not sure what would look good, send me a photo of the room and I will suggest options.

A media wall that makes your TV area the centerpiece

If the TV wall is the one everyone looks at, it is worth doing properly. A media wall turns a bare wall with a TV on it into a feature of the home. It can be built in many ways:

  • With or without shelves. Floating shelves for decor, books and a soundbar, or a clean, minimal wall with just the TV.
  • With or without cabinets. Built-in storage below or beside the TV for media equipment and everyday things.
  • With a fireplace. A fireplace feature with a mantel gives the room a warm focal point.
  • In any color. Painted, stained or natural wood, to match your furniture and floors.

I also take care of the TV itself: it is mounted level and lined up with the design, with neat cord covers so the wall looks finished. You can see real examples in the gallery below.

Questions

Good to know

Can you build a wall to my own design?

Yes. Bring a photo or an idea and I will tell you what is possible and give you a clear estimate. I can also suggest layouts that work well for your TV size.

Is the TV mount included?

I can mount the TV as part of the wall installation, so everything is level and lines up with the design.

How long does an accent wall take?

A simple accent wall can be finished in a day or two. Larger media walls take longer, and I give you a timeline in the estimate.

How much does a media wall cost?

An accent wall with TV mounting and a mantel starts from $1,500. A full media wall is typically $3,500 to $4,000, and large custom projects run $6,000 to $10,000 or more. The exact price depends on size, design and materials. Send the wall width and height with photos for a free estimate.

Can you build a media wall with a fireplace?

Yes. I build the wall around an electric fireplace with a mantel and finish it to match. If a new outlet is needed, a licensed electrician handles that part.

Do I need to know the exact design?

No. Send photos of your room and a reference of a wall you like, and I will suggest layouts, colors and materials.
