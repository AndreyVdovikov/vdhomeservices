Good to know

Can you build a wall to my own design?

Yes. Bring a photo or an idea and I will tell you what is possible and give you a clear estimate. I can also suggest layouts that work well for your TV size.

Is the TV mount included?

I can mount the TV as part of the wall installation, so everything is level and lines up with the design.

How long does an accent wall take?

A simple accent wall can be finished in a day or two. Larger media walls take longer, and I give you a timeline in the estimate.
