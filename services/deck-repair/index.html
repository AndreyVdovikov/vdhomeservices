Questions

Good to know

Is my deck safe to use?

If boards are soft, railings wobble or stairs feel loose, it is time for a repair. I check the structure and tell you honestly what needs to be fixed first.

Do deck repairs need a permit?

Small repairs usually do not, but larger structural work can. I tell you upfront what applies before we start.

Can you stain or seal the deck after repair?

Yes. Sealing or staining protects the wood from Florida sun and rain, and it makes the repair look uniform.
