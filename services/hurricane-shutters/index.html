Questions

Good to know

Which type of shutter is right for my home?

It depends on your budget, how often you want to open and close them, and the openings you need to cover. I explain the options and give you a clear estimate for each one.

Do hurricane shutters need a permit?

Shutter projects often do. I walk you through what applies to your address before we start so there are no surprises.

When should I install them?

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. Booking well before the season gives you the most choice and avoids the last-minute rush.
